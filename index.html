A city, one pixel at a time.

Everything on this block is built in real time. Boxes, low-poly trees, a hand-painted procedural court, and four players with two-bone inverse kinematics. No models, no baked scene images.

3D SCENE → SMALL FRAMEBUFFER
DEPTH EDGES → OKLAB PALETTE
BAYER DITHER → WHOLE PIXELS

The orthographic camera sits 31° above the street. The scene is drawn without antialiasing into a small texture, reduced to a 54-color city palette, then enlarged with exact pixel fetches. Hard shadows keep the shapes crisp.

Rain fills a precomputed map of shallow depressions. The puddles sample a second rendering of the neighborhood, mirrored across the court. Daylight blends into dusk and night over 1.6 seconds.

House rules: 1 point inside, 2 beyond the arc. First to 11. Make it, take it. Check at the top after a basket; clear the arc on a change of possession. Click the handler to shoot, their teammate to pass, or the court to move. Keyboard: S shoot, P pass, Space pause, ← → rotate.
